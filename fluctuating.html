Mundo fluctuante

concept design + graphic design · object design

2024

Fluctuating World is a speculative design project that explores the challenges of a fictional world in which the ground is unstable and constantly moving. Within this imagined reality, the project focuses on a specific issue affecting everyday life: strawberry harvesting in the southern Spanish province of Almería, where this agricultural sector plays a significant role in both the regional and national economy. The project follows a design methodology, the Double Diamond, that uses the design process itself as a tool for investigation. By understanding the root of the problem within this speculative context, the aim is to develop a solution that is both functional and well adapted to the unique conditions of this fluctuating environment.
