D Pata Negra

photography + art direction

2025

Collaboration with Trashvesty. Through its experimental practice, Trashvesty critiques the body as flesh, drawing inspiration from 1970s Spanish destape films, which objectified women. It challenges the notion of the body as mere meat, confronting the gaze that reduces it to an object of consumption. The art direction takes inspiration from the cult Spanish film Jamón, Jamón, directed by Bigas Luna, using its provocative visual language to further explore themes of desire, objectification, and the commodification of the body.

This critical perspective is reflected in a deliberately abrasive visual language, conceived to provoke and resonate within the context of contemporary art. The recurring use of red evokes a sense of violence and desire while simultaneously serving as a cataloguing element that structures the project.
