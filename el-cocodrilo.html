El cocodrilo

editorial design + graphic design + illustration

Design and visual interpretation of the book The Crocodile by Felisberto Hernández.

This project delves into the fantastical and emotional world of The Crocodile, where music, melancholy, and surrealism converge.

The story follows a nomadic pianist who expresses his sadness, turning emotion into performance. His perspective blurs the line between sincerity and manipulation, revealing emotional detachment.

The illustrations are collages combining my own photographs, found images, and hand-drawn elements, evoking a dreamlike atmosphere rooted in magical realism.

The layout is based on the Van de Graaf canon, offering generous white space that amplifies the surreal tone of the visuals. The Sabon typeface, tested at 10pt and 11pt, adds clarity and elegance, enhancing the introspective nature of the work.
