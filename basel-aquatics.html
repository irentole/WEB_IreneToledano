BASEL AQUATICS Logo Proposal.

In 2021, following the Tokyo Olympics, the sport known as synchronized swimming was renamed artistic swimming and the historic organization FINA changed its name to World Aquatics. This change prompted clubs, organizations, and federations to adopt new branding, leading to modifications in names and visual identities.

As Basel Aquatics is a historic club for the city, I believe it is essential to maintain a connection with Basel’s logo while giving it a modern touch. Additionally, it should clearly convey its identity as a sports club focused on water-based activities. It combines the city logo and a swimmer.
