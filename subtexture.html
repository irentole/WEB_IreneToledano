SubTexture

editorial design

2026

This editorial design project was developed as part of the Master's Degree at LABASAD. It explores the hidden structures of fashion, focusing on textures, materials, and the people behind them as the defining elements of its identity.

Through a sensitive, documentary approach, the publication aims to reveal the beauty and value of what often goes unnoticed. It celebrates the professionals and artisans whose expertise shapes each piece, highlighting their contribution to the creative process. The magazine serves both as a showcase for these stories and as a source of inspiration.

The current issue focuses on artistic swimming, and work on the next edition is already underway.

Disclaimer: All editorial texts are fictional and have been generated with AI based on existing interviews and publicly available sources. They have been created exclusively for educational purposes. The objective of this project is to demonstrate the editorial design process, including concept development, layout, typography, and visual editing, rather than to present original journalistic content.

While this publication is currently an academic project, the intention is to develop it into a real publication featuring original investigations into the hidden layers of fashion.
