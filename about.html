About

Irene Toledano

Hi! I’m Irene, a multidisciplinary designer with a love for object, concept, graphic design, and photography.

I develop creative projects that draw on my artisanal background and audiovisual culture to shape compelling visual solutions.

As an artistic swimmer and dancer, I bring a strong artistic sensibility to my work. Seeing design through movement and choreography allows me to approach forms, lines, and spaces with a sense of rhythm, fluidity, and dynamic expression. This connection between design and sport shapes both my creative process and the way I collaborate with others.

My experience spans graphic design, photography, and performance. As an art and photography assistant at LUZIRT, I worked within a studio specialising in advertising photography. Alongside this, I have developed freelance editorial and visual projects, from books and fanzines to photography and illustration, exploring how different visual languages can tell a story.

Since 2018, I have also performed as a principal swimmer and dancer in Dragon Rests Your Head on the Seabed, a piece by Pablo Esbert and Federico Strate presented across Europe. My work in artistic swimming, from coaching to choreographic composition, continues to inform my design practice, bringing together visual thinking, physical expression, and a collaborative spirit.
