MHT chairs

object design + photography · graphic design

2024

Object Design Project in collaboration with Haizea Milán Aparicio and Marian Tadeo Salas.

It began as part of the course Objects II in the Design Degree at UCM, led by Andrea Santamaría, but has continued as an independent project with an eco-design perspective.

Currently, it is still in development alongside a project that seeks to find a method for creating recycled plastic sheets to be used in building the chairs.

Inspired by Hieronymus Bosch’s The Garden of Earthly Delights, the MHT chairs celebrate variability and spontaneity.

They seek a creative approach to standard production methods, where dimensions are usually precise and predefined.

The heating and bending of the material do not follow a strict script. Instead, they allow creativity to emerge as we respond to the unique properties of each piece.
