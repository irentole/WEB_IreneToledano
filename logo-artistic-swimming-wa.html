Artistic Swimming WA Logo

visual identity

2022

In 2021, following the Tokyo Olympics, the sport known as synchronized swimming was renamed artistic swimming, and the historic organization FINA changed its name to World Aquatics. This change prompted clubs, organizations, and federations to adopt new branding, leading to modifications in names and visual identities.

Below are projects for Artistic Swimming WA, the artistic swimming federation of Western Australia, and Basel Aquatics.

The logo consists of three elements: the black swan, symbolizing Western Australia; three synchronized, pointed legs, representing the sport; and a composition that forms a water droplet, symbolizing the aquatic environment in which the sport is performed.
