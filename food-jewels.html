Food Jewels

object design + art direction + photography

2024

Project led by Andrea Santamaría as part of the Design Object course in the Design Degree at the Faculty of Fine Arts, Complutense University of Madrid (UCM), in collaboration with Eve Robles.

In this project, fruits and vegetables are carefully transformed into ephemeral art jewellery pieces. The project stems from a desire to understand and communicate our relationship with food, offering a critical exploration of the contemporary circumstances that shape the way we produce, consume, and value it.

Inspired by the myth of Narcissus, Food Jewels piece no. 2 invites us to question and explore our obsession with beauty, perfection, and the desire to belong. They reflect on how contemporary capitalist culture makes us constantly pursue more beauty, success, and consumption.
